Misbaha

A calm tasbih counter. No ads while you count.

Misbaha is a tasbih (dhikr) counter: a ring of amber beads that lights up as you count, the tasbih after prayer (33 · 33 · 34), your own dhikr, and a daily history. The counting screen never shows ads.
